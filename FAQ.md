# Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common ToneCustoms customer questions. For product-specific behavior, see the individual product guide.

## Buying and availability

### Where can I purchase ToneCustoms scripts?
Use the [official ToneCustoms Tebex storefront](https://tonecustoms.tebex.store/). A GitHub page or YouTube showcase does not by itself mean a script is for sale.

### How do I receive a purchased script?
Use the fulfillment and entitlement instructions provided by the **specific product listing, checkout confirmation, and delivery email**. Delivery may differ between products. See the [Purchase & Delivery Guide](PURCHASE_AND_DELIVERY.md).

### Does every script support Qbox, QBCore and ESX?
No universal compatibility promise is made. Each product specifies its own supported environments and dependencies. Read the [Compatibility Guide](COMPATIBILITY.md) and the applicable product page.

## Using your purchase

### Can I use a purchased script on more than one server?
ToneCustoms' proposed licensing approach permits use on **multiple FiveM servers you own**, subject to the purchase terms accepted at checkout and any applicable distribution or third-party restrictions. [Licensing details](policies/LICENSING.md).

### May my developer install a script for me?
An authorized developer may assist with installation and allowed configuration for your servers. They may not redistribute the script or use it independently.

### Can I change the script?
The intended permission covers **designated configuration files only**. Modifying core logic, NUI, or other files requires ToneCustoms approval. Consult your actual purchase terms.

### How do I install a product?
Start at the [Installation Hub](INSTALLATION.md). [Gun Jam's guide](products/gun-jam-installation.md) documents its specific item setup and dependencies.

## Updates and support

### Are updates free?
**Published updates** to scripts you purchased are provided without an additional update charge under ToneCustoms' documented business approach, subject to actual checkout terms. There is **no guaranteed update schedule**. [Update details](policies/UPDATES.md).

### Will updating overwrite my settings?
That depends on the delivered package. Preserve your allowed configuration values and follow the product's release notes before replacing files. [Safe Update Guide](UPDATE_GUIDE.md).

### Is support free?
Product installation, configuration and troubleshooting assistance are free through [ToneCustoms Discord](https://discord.gg/JdZ7NTG5KF); responses are not guaranteed instantly or 24/7.

### Can I swap a script for another?
You may **request** a swap for another ToneCustoms script of reasonably comparable value, using the amount originally paid as a consideration. Approval is discretionary, and no automatic exchange or cash difference is promised. [Swap Policy](policies/SCRIPT_SWAPS.md).

### What if I want a refund?
Refund rights and procedures depend on the terms presented at checkout, platform requirements, and applicable law. See [Purchases & Refunds](policies/PURCHASES_AND_REFUNDS.md).

## Contact and security

### How can I report a bug?
Use the [Contact & Feedback Guide](CONTACT.md). Include product name, framework, expected/actual behavior and redacted logs.

### Should I post license keys or purchase details in GitHub Issues?
No. Do not publish receipts, order IDs, license keys, account credentials or protected code. Use an appropriate **private** support channel for purchase verification.
