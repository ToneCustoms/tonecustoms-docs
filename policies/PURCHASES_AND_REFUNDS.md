# ToneCustoms — Purchases, Delivery & Refunds

**Store alignment review: October 24, 2026**

This page records the current **ToneCustoms business approach** and the **refund-policy selection observed in its Tebex dashboard**. It does not replace the policy and legal terms displayed by Tebex during checkout, or applicable consumer rights.

## Official purchasing and delivery

The official store is [tonecustoms.tebex.store](https://tonecustoms.tebex.store/). Review each package's features, compatibility, requirements, delivery method and license information **before purchasing**.

The live ToneCustoms listings reviewed for **Family System** and **Window Cleaning** describe delivery through **Cfx.re Keymaster / Asset Escrow**. Delivery details must still be checked per package and order. See the [Purchase & Delivery Guide](../PURCHASE_AND_DELIVERY.md).

## Refund policy actually selected in Tebex

During the October 24, 2026 dashboard review, the selected setting at **Tebex Creator Dashboard → Project → Refund Policy** was:

**[Default] No Refunds Policy**

This aligns with ToneCustoms' decision **not to offer discretionary refunds for a change of mind**. It does **not** mean that delivery failures, inaccessible products, statutory remedies, payment-provider rules, or Tebex buyer-support decisions can be ignored.

Tebex describes its default no-refunds approach as having exceptions where the product is not delivered or a technical problem prevents product access. The actual Tebex refund policy and checkout terms govern how requests are handled. [Tebex creator documentation](https://docs.tebex.io/creators/tebex-control-panel/settings/project).

**Wording caveat:** Tebex's displayed default policy text refers generically to virtual items, platform bans and server shutdowns. ToneCustoms sells FiveM development resources, so this generic text is not a tailored script-specific license description. The dashboard selection is confirmed; it has **not** been changed or replaced through this documentation update.

## Support, returns and order problems

1. For technical installation and configuration support, contact [ToneCustoms Discord](https://discord.gg/JdZ7NTG5KF).
2. For billing/refund questions, use the order's official Tebex buyer-support route and follow any existing support thread.
3. If you open an order-related case with Tebex, keep relevant replies within that Tebex support thread so the platform can review them.
4. Share order references **only in an appropriate private channel**. Never publish payment information, login credentials, license keys or private protected source code.

ToneCustoms provides installation, configuration and product troubleshooting assistance without an additional support charge. No 24/7 service or instant response guarantee is made.

## Licensing and updates

Under the ToneCustoms owner-defined approach, purchasers may use a script on multiple FiveM servers **they own** and may modify **designated configuration files only**. Unauthorized resale, redistribution and sharing are not permitted. Authorized developers may assist with installation/configuration on the purchaser's servers, without obtaining an independent license.

Approved script updates are provided free when released; no fixed release cadence is promised. For specific permissions and any platform conditions, review [Licensing](LICENSING.md), [Updates](UPDATES.md) and the terms accepted at checkout.

## Optional ToneCustoms script swaps

Customers may **request**, but are not entitled to, a swap to another ToneCustoms resource of reasonably comparable value. Value is assessed in part against **what the customer actually paid**, and each request needs ToneCustoms approval. There are no automatic cash credits, refunds, or customer-to-customer trades. See the [Script Swap Policy](SCRIPT_SWAPS.md).

Discretionary swaps do not replace legal rights or Tebex buyer remedies.
