# ToneCustoms — Purchases, Delivery & Refund Information

**Status: Proposed policy draft — not approved checkout or refund terms.**

## Official purchasing channel

Verify current offerings and purchase conditions through the [official ToneCustoms Tebex store](https://tonecustoms.tebex.store/). The existence of a GitHub repository, developer showcase or YouTube video does **not** mean the item is on sale.

## Information to provide on each listing

Before a resource is offered for sale, its listing should clearly identify:

- What is included and the actual working feature set
- Supported frameworks, minimum dependencies and known compatibility limits
- Delivery method, access requirements and setup instructions
- Whether configuration/customization services are included
- License scope and any applicable restrictions
- Refund/cancellation rules shown at checkout
- Update and support entitlements, if offered

## Delivery

**To be confirmed:** Whether each product is delivered by Tebex asset entitlement, an escrow-protected resource, direct download, manual fulfillment, or another approved channel. Do not promise a delivery method or delivery time before it is verified for that product.

## Refunds and cancellations

**No uniform ToneCustoms refund policy has been approved in this document.** Purchases are subject to the actual checkout terms, relevant provider rules and applicable consumer law. Nothing in this draft waives statutory consumer protections.

For purchase-specific questions, use the support route specified on your order or the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/JdZ7NTG5KF), without sharing payment credentials publicly.

## Problems with an order

When seeking assistance, provide the purchased product name, approximate purchase date, the nature of the issue and any non-sensitive order reference **only through an appropriate private support channel**.

Do not post payment information, authentication tokens or private access links in public GitHub issues.
