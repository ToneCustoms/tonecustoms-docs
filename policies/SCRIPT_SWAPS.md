# ToneCustoms — Script Swap Policy

**Business rules recorded · Draft for final checkout/policy alignment**

ToneCustoms may consider requests to exchange a previously purchased ToneCustoms script for another **ToneCustoms** script. Script swaps are **optional and require ToneCustoms approval**. They are not guaranteed purchase benefits or automatic refunds.

## Eligibility

- **ToneCustoms products only.** No third-party scripts or customer-to-customer trading.
- **Comparable value.** The replacement should be reasonably comparable in value to **what the customer actually paid** for the original script. ToneCustoms also considers the requested script's value when deciding whether a swap is fair.
- **No fixed price band or automatic price-difference payment option.** A significantly more valuable script is not an automatic exchange just because a customer offers to pay a difference.
- **Proof of purchase.** The customer must provide enough information through a private support channel for the purchase and license to be confirmed.
- **Owner approval.** ToneCustoms may approve or decline each request based on the circumstances and actual product values.

## How to request a swap

1. Open a private support request through the [official ToneCustoms Discord](https://discord.gg/JdZ7NTG5KF).
2. Identify the originally purchased script and the requested replacement.
3. Provide purchase verification **privately** when requested; never post transaction or account details in public GitHub issues.
4. ToneCustoms reviews eligibility and comparable value and confirms its decision in writing.
5. **Only after approval**, ToneCustoms coordinates any license change and delivery arrangements. The original license must be handled consistently with the approved exchange and relevant distribution-platform rules.

## What swaps do not provide

- No guaranteed exchange just because a request was submitted.
- No automatic store credit, balance refund, or exchange of a significantly more expensive resource.
- No permission to trade scripts with other customers or redistribute paid files.
- No automatic license transfer before approval.

## Refunds and legal rights

A script swap is separate from refund rights and **does not restrict any rights or remedies required by applicable law, checkout agreements, or relevant payment-provider/platform policies**. For purchase issues and refund information, see [Purchases & Refunds](PURCHASES_AND_REFUNDS.md).
