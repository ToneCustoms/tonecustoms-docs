# ToneCustoms — Customer Information & Policy Center

**Customer help, purchasing information and policy reference**

This section explains how customers can learn about ToneCustoms products, request support, and understand the publication and approval process for commercial terms.

> **Terms status:** ToneCustoms has defined its intended licensing, free released-updates, free support and discretionary comparable-value swap approach. These pages document those decisions, but they **do not supersede the actual terms accepted at Tebex checkout**, third-party licenses or applicable law. Privacy practices and some order/delivery details still require review.

- Confirm the current Tebex checkout terms and asset-delivery disclosures are consistent with customer-facing policy wording.
- Confirm legal/provider requirements for refunds, licensing and script swaps.
- Verify data collection and retention practices before publishing a final privacy notice.
- Keep package descriptions and available features aligned with the resources actually delivered.
