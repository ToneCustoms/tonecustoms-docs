# ToneCustoms — Customer Information & Policy Center

**Official information hub · Documentation in progress**

This section explains how customers can learn about ToneCustoms products, request support, and understand the publication and approval process for commercial terms.

> **Policy status — review required:** Licensing, refunds, update entitlements, commercial permissions, support commitments, and privacy details below are **proposed policy drafts** for owner review. They **are not final sales terms or amendments to existing purchases**. For any purchase, refer to the terms actually presented by the official store/checkout and applicable law. No customer should rely on an unapproved draft as an entitlement or restriction.

## What remains to be decided before publishing binding policies

- Exact license scope: per-server, per-customer, permitted modifications, transfers and team access
- Source distribution and resale restrictions consistent with upstream licenses
- What purchases include and how buyers receive them
- Refund/cancellation and optional script-swap procedure consistent with checkout-provider terms and applicable consumer law
- Length and scope of included updates, if any
- Support channels, coverage, response expectations and exclusions
- Data collection and retention practices for any website, licensing tools, bots, or customer support systems

This documentation is not legal advice and does not replace the terms displayed to customers at checkout.
