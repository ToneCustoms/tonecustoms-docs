**Licensed use — draft terms for checkout alignment**

- A purchaser may use the purchased ToneCustoms script on multiple FiveM servers that **they own**, subject to any relevant platform or third-party license restrictions.
- Customers may edit **designated configuration files only** (such as `config.lua`). Changes to core source, UI or protected files require ToneCustoms approval.
- Sharing, leaking, reselling, sublicensing or transferring the purchased script to someone else is not permitted without explicit authorization where enforceable.
- Authorized developers may assist with installation/configuration for the purchaser's servers; they may not take copies for unrelated projects.

**Updates & support**

- Updates that ToneCustoms releases for a purchased script are supplied without an additional update charge under the intended policy. **No release timetable, guaranteed future features, or fixed development cadence.**
- ToneCustoms offers installation and configuration assistance and product support through its official Discord. **No guaranteed instant response or 24/7 staffed service** is promised.

**Purchases and swaps — draft terms**

- The intended business policy is **no refunds for change of mind**, with exceptions/remedies where required by applicable law, checkout or payment-provider rules.
- A customer may **request** an exchange for another **ToneCustoms** script of **reasonably comparable value**, considering the actual amount paid. Swaps are discretionary, not guaranteed, and require owner approval; no automatic cash credit or upgrade is implied.

**Important:** The buyer must see the final approved license, refund, delivery and other contractual conditions at checkout. This template does not amend the actual Tebex terms.
