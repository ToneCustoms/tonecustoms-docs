## Long description — proposed storefront copy

### Make every shot count

**ToneCustoms Gun Jam** is a custom FiveM gameplay resource built around weapon jams and hands-on clearing interactions. Designed to add a moment of tension to gunplay and give weapon handling an additional roleplay mechanic.

### Feature overview

The following describes the **development specification**, not an independently verified release feature list:

- Weapon-shot tracking with a configurable/defined jam mechanic in the project.
- A weapon-clearing interaction and associated animation.
- Weapon category support and exclusions managed through the resource configuration.
- Integration requirements depending on the approved build.

**Do not advertise the precise shot interval, supported weapons, repair-kit requirements or animation details as shipped features until confirmed with the actual build.**
