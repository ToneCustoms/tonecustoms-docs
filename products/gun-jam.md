# ToneCustoms Gun Jam

**Category:** Gameplay / weapon interaction  
**Publication status:** Showcase documentation; public release availability not confirmed here  
**Demonstration:** [Watch Gun Jam](https://youtu.be/kPniOo-VThw)

## Overview

ToneCustoms Gun Jam explores an immersive firearm-jamming mechanic that creates an additional interaction during gunplay. The development work includes shot tracking and a clearing action.

## Demonstrated / described workflow

- Firearm usage can trigger a jam based on the configured mechanic.
- The player performs a clearing action before normal firing resumes.
- Weapon support and exclusions are handled through resource configuration.

> Exact weapon compatibility, counts, animations, inventory integration and other behavior are **build-specific**. They should not be assumed from this public overview.
