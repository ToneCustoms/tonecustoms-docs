## Overview

ToneCustoms Pharmacy is a FiveM resource project involving pharmacy interactions and an activity-based rehabilitation experience. Instead of representing the in-game rehabilitation sequence as a single generic progress circle, the intended gameplay uses actual character exercise actions.

## Planned / under-development features

- Pharmacy interactions and item-related functionality.
- An interactive rehabilitation counselor accessed through **ox_target**.
- Recovery exercises such as push-ups, sit-ups and cardio/running.
- Animated exercise sequences with repetition or checkpoint validation, fatigue or stamina effects and feedback.
- Persisted progress, retry and completion behaviors as part of the planned activity design.

**These are development requirements, not verified released functionality.** In-game rehabilitation is a fictional gameplay mechanic and not medical treatment advice.
