## Understand feature status

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Concept** | An idea being explored, not a commitment |
| **In development** | Work underway; not necessarily customer-ready |
| **Preview** | Demonstration or early look, not proof of release |
| **Released** | Confirmed customer-facing availability through an official listing or delivery |

The presence of a product page or video **does not establish current store availability**. Check the [official Tebex store](https://tonecustoms.tebex.store/) before purchasing.

## Suggest a feature

Have an improvement in mind? [Submit a feature request](https://github.com/ToneCustoms/tonecustoms-docs/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml). Ideas are reviewed individually; there is no guaranteed release schedule.
