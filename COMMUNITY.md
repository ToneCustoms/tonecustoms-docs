## What to expect

### Exclusive-feeling insights, without paywalls or invented promises

Developer Insights is a public **community-first** area for previews, roadmaps-in-progress, feature spotlights, polls or feedback invitations. Posts may be marked *Concept*, *In development*, *Preview*, or *Released*. A preview is not a purchase promise or release date.

### Get involved

Share gameplay feedback, suggest features, watch showcases and join discussions through the official Discord. Public GitHub Issues are for structured support and suggestions—not an open chat room. The documentation site does not currently provide its own accounts, comments or member-only area.

### Community standards

Keep discussions constructive and respect other creators. Do not share purchased scripts, private code, credentials, personal information or misleading partnership claims. Read [Rules & Regulations](RULES_AND_REGULATIONS.md).

## Stay informed

Bookmark [Announcements](ANNOUNCEMENTS.md) for official news and [Developer Insights](INSIGHTS.md) for early looks. You can also follow ToneCustoms on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@tonyiswavy12) and join [Discord](https://discord.gg/JdZ7NTG5KF).

**Note:** No private subscriber perks, early-access downloads or special memberships are being promised by this page.
