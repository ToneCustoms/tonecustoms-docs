# ToneCustoms — Rules & Regulations

**Official conduct and resource-use reference · Built for Roleplay. Made with Purpose.**

These guidelines explain how to use ToneCustoms support, documentation, partnerships and purchased FiveM resources responsibly. They summarize existing published policies; they **do not replace** the terms accepted at Tebex checkout, a signed agreement, applicable law, or third-party licenses. Product-specific requirements take precedence where applicable.

## 1. Script purchases and permitted use

- Use purchased ToneCustoms resources on FiveM servers **you own**, subject to the applicable purchase and license terms. The owner-defined licensing approach permits multiple servers owned by the same purchaser.
- Authorized developers may help install and configure a resource for the purchaser's servers; this does not grant them a separate license.
- Change designated configuration files as allowed by the resource documentation. Obtain permission before modifying restricted core files where the applicable license requires it.
- Do not resell, leak, share, sublicense, redistribute or transfer paid scripts, protected source code, keys or license access without appropriate authorization.
- Respect any third-party open-source licenses, platform terms and escrow restrictions.

See [Licensing](policies/LICENSING.md) and [Purchase & Delivery](PURCHASE_AND_DELIVERY.md).

## 2. Purchasing, refunds and exchanges

- Confirm current product availability, features, compatibility and delivery through the [official Tebex store](https://tonecustoms.tebex.store/).
- Read the actual checkout terms before purchasing. A demonstration or documentation page does not establish a purchasable release.
- ToneCustoms does not offer discretionary change-of-mind refunds under its selected Tebex default no-refunds approach. Applicable legal rights, provider rules and remedies for delivery/access problems still apply.
- Script swaps are **requests**, not entitlements. Approval depends on comparable value, including the amount originally paid; no automatic cash credit is promised.
- Do not post payment details, order references or license keys in public GitHub Issues. Use the original private Tebex order-support route for billing, refunds and order verification.

See [Purchases & Refunds](policies/PURCHASES_AND_REFUNDS.md) and [Script Swaps](policies/SCRIPT_SWAPS.md).

## 3. Support and community conduct

- Treat customers, creators, developers and staff respectfully. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, fraud, spam and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.
- Submit accurate, relevant technical reports. Include the product, environment, reproduction steps and **redacted** error messages when available.
- Do not publish passwords, tokens, personal information, customer records, protected scripts or security-sensitive details.
- Use the [public GitHub Support Center](https://github.com/ToneCustoms/tonecustoms-docs/issues/new/choose) for technical and documentation issues. GitHub Issues are public and require a GitHub account.
- Use the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for sensitive vulnerability reports, not a public issue.
- Support submissions are accepted at any time; published staffed hours and the **24–48-hour initial response target** are not guaranteed response or resolution deadlines.
- Suggestions and requests do not guarantee implementation, immediate attention or future release dates.

See [Support](SUPPORT.md) and [Customer Support Policy](policies/CUSTOMER_SUPPORT.md).

## 4. Partnerships, promotions and brand use

- Submit only non-confidential information through the [Partnership Inquiry](PARTNERSHIPS.md).
- Do not falsely claim to represent, sponsor, employ or speak for ToneCustoms.
- Do not use ToneCustoms logos, protected assets, promotional materials or customer testimonials beyond permissions actually granted.
- Partnership discussions do not automatically provide free scripts, discounts, priority support, sponsorship, exclusivity, revenue share or redistribution rights.
- Agree in writing on deliverables, compensation (if any), intellectual property, publicity, confidentiality, duration and termination before making commitments.
- Follow applicable advertising disclosure requirements, FiveM/Cfx.re terms and third-party rights.

See [Partnership Rules](PARTNERSHIPS.md#partnership-rules-and-regulations) and the [Agreement Planning Template](PARTNERSHIP_AGREEMENT_TEMPLATE.md).

## 5. Custom development and intellectual property

- A development inquiry is not an accepted order or promise of availability, price, deadline or delivery.
- Scope, requirements, dependencies, acceptance criteria, ownership and commercial terms must be explicitly agreed for each accepted project.
- Do not submit assets or source code you lack permission to use.
- Private repositories are for development, not general customer downloads.

See [Custom Development](CUSTOM_DEVELOPMENT.md).

## 6. Enforcement and reporting

ToneCustoms may review public submissions, decline proposals, moderate inappropriate participation and enforce applicable license or agreement rights in accordance with platform rules and law. Any action concerning a purchased product remains subject to the customer's applicable terms and legal rights; this page does not create a new unilateral right to revoke purchases.

For a technical issue, use [GitHub Support](https://github.com/ToneCustoms/tonecustoms-docs/issues/new/choose). For a private order dispute, use the original Tebex order-support route. For sensitive security disclosures, follow [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Policy status and precedence

These are operational guidelines and cross-references, **not a new checkout contract**. The [Licensing Policy](policies/LICENSING.md) and [Privacy & Data Handling](policies/PRIVACY.md) still identify items awaiting final review. A signed partnership or development agreement, applicable product license, Tebex checkout terms, third-party rights and law must be evaluated separately.
