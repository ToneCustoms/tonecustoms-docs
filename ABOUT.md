# About ToneCustoms

**Built for Roleplay. Made with Purpose.**

ToneCustoms is an independent FiveM development brand creating custom resources and interactive gameplay systems for roleplay servers. Our work is informed by first-hand experience developing and operating FiveM communities.

## What we create

| Area | Our focus |
| --- | --- |
| Gameplay systems | Interactive mechanics that add variety to roleplay |
| Civilian activities | Jobs and activities that players can actively take part in |
| Server systems | Configurable tools that support a server's daily operations |
| Weapon interactions | Equipment mechanics with practical in-game interactions |

Each resource has its own requirements and compatibility. Product pages explain what a specific script offers.

## Our approach

**Build with purpose.** A feature should serve the player or server owner, not exist just to make a feature list longer.

**Prioritize working functionality.** New versions and announcements follow actual approved releases, not every code change.

**Document what customers need.** Installation requirements, configuration, demonstrations and support information should be easy to locate.

**Listen and improve.** Feedback helps guide fixes and future ideas. Updates to purchased scripts are free when released, without a fixed release schedule.
