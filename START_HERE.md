## Before choosing a script

**1. Identify your server's needs.** Are you looking for a civilian job, a player interaction system, a weapon mechanic, or a server-management tool?

**2. Read the product page.** Review the described features and the specific framework, dependency, or installation requirements where documented.

**3. Confirm current availability.** Some resources have development pages or video demonstrations but are not necessarily available to purchase. The [official Tebex store](https://tonecustoms.tebex.store/) is the source for current listings and checkout terms.

**4. Review customer information.** Read the relevant [licensing and purchase policies](policies/README.md) before ordering.

**5. Use the official support route.** For product questions or installation assistance, use the [GitHub Support Center](https://github.com/ToneCustoms/tonecustoms-docs/issues/new/choose). For billing or order-support cases, continue through the original Tebex support channel.

## Already own a ToneCustoms resource?

Start with the [Installation Hub](INSTALLATION.md) and your product-specific guide. If you encounter a problem, check the applicable troubleshooting page or use the [Support Guide](SUPPORT.md).

When requesting help, identify the resource, your framework and dependencies, expected behavior, actual behavior, and relevant **redacted** errors. Never post passwords, license keys, payment information, private source code, or other sensitive details in public.

## About our GitHub organization

The [ToneCustoms GitHub organization](https://github.com/ToneCustoms) houses public documentation and private development repositories. **Private script repositories are not customer download locations.** Customers should follow their official product delivery instructions.
